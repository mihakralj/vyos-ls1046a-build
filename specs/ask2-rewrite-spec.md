**Revision note (2026-07-14):** This spec's §11.1 flow-ceiling numbers and MURAM budget assumptions derive from the Fork-A/OH-port era (pre-July 2026). The current Fork-B FE-VM ehash path (silicon-verified July 6-13) changes the ceiling model: ehash buckets and flow records live in DDR, not MURAM. Under nexthop dedup (patch 0120, `fman_hm_nexthop_get/put`), only nexthop count (~200-400 distinct {egress, smac, dmac} tuples) consumes MURAM — flow count is unbounded. The 327× `-ENOMEM` and 750-flow ceiling referenced in §11.1/§16 are Fork-A artifacts. Similarly, EKFC=0x001C0006 (13-byte 5-tuple) is the confirmed ehash key format; extraction order is MSB-first (SIP+DIP+PROTO+SPORT+DPORT), confirmed by CRC-64 hash-match 2026-07-13. See `specs/fman-keygen-flow-key-spec.md` v3.1 and `arch/fman-microcode-210-programming-reference.md` v1.0 for current silicon facts. Upcoming P1 items (multi-FQ ENQ spread, eth3 PHY fix) and P2 items (HM in HIT chain, soft parser) are not yet reflected in this spec's milestone plan.
